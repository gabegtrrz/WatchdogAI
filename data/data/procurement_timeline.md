# # Procurement Timeline Breakdown

A verified overview of the procurement process based on the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of R.A. 9184.

Government Procurement Policy Board, “The Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 12009,” Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines, Feb. 10, 2025. [Online]. Available: https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/downloads/2025/02feb/20250210-IRR-RA-12009-FRM.pdf

---

### **1. Strategic Procurement Planning**

- **Stage:** Pre-procurement
- **Purpose:** Requires procuring entities to conduct market scoping, assess supplier capabilities, and align strategies with industry conditions to optimize resource allocation.
- **Legal Timeline (IRR):** Must be completed before the issuance of the Annual Procurement Plan (APP). No specific duration is mandated, as it is part of the entity's internal annual planning cycle. For complex projects, this may involve extensive feasibility studies or market analysis.

---

### **2. Posting of Procurement Opportunity**

- **Stage:** Pre-bidding
- **Purpose:** Publicly announces procurement opportunities to attract potential bidders or suppliers.
- **Legal Timeline (IRR):**
    - **Competitive Bidding:** The Invitation to Bid must be posted continuously for at least **7 calendar days** on PhilGEPS, the entity's website, and in a conspicuous location.
    - **Competitive Dialogue:** The Request for Expression of Interest (REOI) is posted for at least **7 calendar days**.
    - **Direct Contracting & Limited Source Bidding:** Public posting is generally not required.

---

### **3. Submission of Bids or Proposals**

- **Stage:** Bidding
- **Purpose:** Deadline for bidders to submit their technical and financial proposals.
- **Legal Timeline (IRR):**
    - **Competitive Bidding:** The deadline is set within the advertisement period. For projects with an Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC) of **₱50 million or less**, this is typically on the 7th calendar day of posting. For projects with an ABC **above ₱50 million**, the period is significantly longer to allow for adequate bid preparation.
    - **Alternative Modalities:** Timelines are set by the procuring entity, often ranging from 7 to 14 calendar days after the dialogue or issuance of the Request for Quotation.

---

### **4. Bid Evaluation and Opening**

- **Stage:** Bidding
- **Purpose:** Publicly opens and evaluates bids to determine the Lowest Calculated Responsive Bid (LCRB) or Highest Rated Responsive Bid (HRRB).
- **Legal Timeline (IRR):**
    - **Competitive Bidding:** The bid opening occurs **immediately** after the submission deadline. The detailed evaluation must be completed within a maximum of **7 calendar days**.
    - **Alternative Modalities:** Evaluation timelines are determined by the procuring entity but typically range from 5 to 14 calendar days depending on complexity.

---

### **5. Issuance of Notice of Award**

- **Stage:** Post-bidding (Awarding)
- **Purpose:** Notifies the winning bidder and publicizes the award decision to ensure transparency.
- **Legal Timeline (IRR):** To be issued within **7 calendar days** from the date of the Bids and Awards Committee (BAC) resolution recommending the award. This may be extended up to 15 calendar days with valid justification. This timeline generally applies across all modalities.

---

### **6. Contract Signing and Approval**

- **Stage:** Post-awarding (Contracting)
- **Purpose:** Formalizes the agreement between the procuring entity and the winning bidder.
- **Legal Timeline (IRR):** The winning bidder must sign the contract within **10 calendar days** from receiving the Notice of Award. This is consistent across most procurement modalities.
