Understanding the Senstivity Parameter

The sensitivity (a.k.a. contamination) parameter tells the AI model how many anomalies to expect in the data.

Conservative (0.01-0.1):
• Fewer false alarms
• May miss some issues
• Good for clean datasets
Sensitive (0.2-0.5):
• Catches more potential issues
• May flag normal transactions
• Good for high-risk scenarios

Tip: Start with 0.05 (5%) and adjust based on your results. If you find too many false positives, try a lower value. If you're missing obvious issues, try a higher contamination value.
