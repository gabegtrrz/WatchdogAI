# Objectives

1. To design and implement a tunable data generation framework capable of simulating realistic and anomalous procurement transactions, thereby providing a controlled and comprehensive dataset essential for training and rigorously evaluating the anomaly detection model.
2. To develop and optimize an AI-driven anomaly detection system using the Isolation Forest algorithm, in order to automatically identify anomalous data points that are potentially fraudulent or erroneous transactions. This involves rigorous feature engineering to construct the most impactful data representations and systematic hyperparameter tuning to configure the Isolation Forest model for peak accuracy, supported by a data augmentation component that integrates real-time market price data.
3. To construct an Explainable AI (XAI) component utilizing SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), in order to demystify the model's decision-making process by providing clear, feature-level explanations for why a transaction is flagged as anomalous, thereby enhancing transparency and user trust.
4. To implement a simulated blockchain environment, in order to demonstrate the potential of core blockchain principles to enhance the data integrity and auditability of procurement records, serving as a proof-of-concept for securing transaction data against unauthorized modifications.
5. To integrate the anomaly detection system, XAI component, and simulated blockchain into a cohesive web application, in order to provide a user-friendly and accessible interface that enables non-technical stakeholders to upload, analyze, and interpret procurement data.
6. To conduct a multi-faceted evaluation of the integrated system, in order to quantitatively validate its overall effectiveness, performance, and quality by assessing model accuracy, measuring computational efficiency to ensure scalability, and evaluating software quality based on the ISO/IEC 25010 standard to confirm its suitability for end-users.
