Understanding the Sensitivity Option

Think of the sensitivity setting as a suspicion filter 🕵️.

A low setting is very strict, only showing the absolute most suspicious purchases. A high setting casts a wider net, flagging a larger group of potentially unusual transactions for review.

For example, a setting of 5% will show the 5 most unusual transactions out of 100.

Conservative (0.01-0.1):
• More precise, fewer false alarms
• May miss some actual anomalies
• Good for clean datasets
Sensitive (0.2-0.5):
• Catches more potential issues
• May capture non-anomalous transactions
• Good for high-risk scenarios
